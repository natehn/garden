*CW: suicide, non-graphic*

*In answer to David, and the words of Oriah Mountain Dreamer.*

~

When I touch the center of my sorrow    
There is Carl    
And an emptiness    
Where his guidance,     
And smile,     
And presence should be.

And there are questions:    
Why?    
Why him?    
Why then?

I remember when I *heard* -    
My heart beating out of my chest,     
Up into my ears.    
Eighteen years old, and -     
For some reason -     
Calling every person I knew, saying:    
Carl is gone,    
Carl is gone,     
Carl is gone.

And all these years later,    
I’m still looking up at him    
And he, still telling me    
That God has a plan.    
That God has a *great* plan for my life.    
Could he not believe the same for himself?

Could he not have lived long, grown old - or rather, older -    
Could he not have met his grandchildren    
Could he not have come round again, when I finished college, to check in 

I figured that maybe all these plans weren’t worth so much    
If someone like him could be left behind    
And so I sought out some other plan    
One without Suicide, without    
Giving Up.

And eventually, as time passed, I put Carl away.    
I hid him right in the center -     
Layer by layer,    
Buffering the pain, cloaking the trigger.    
The one sorrow I do not touch,     
For it is the one I least understand.

You see, even now    
I fear becoming some sort of “Evan Hansen” character.     
I wasn’t his best friend, I wasn’t kin.        
I don’t have any photos of us.    
I can barely remember his face anymore. 

But the truth is, when I lost him,     
Something else died, too.    
And I have this feeling     
That I’ll spend the rest of my life    
Searching for it,    
And wondering     
What it really was.

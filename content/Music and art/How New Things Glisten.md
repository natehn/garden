She wrote that everything changes    
Her mantra tattooed on my wrist    
That to let go is not so dangerous    
Compared to counting what’s missed

Despite that thoughtful counsel    
I find it hard to embrace    
I want things as they are, though imperfect    
At least then I know the pace

But the practice now is to listen     
To sit, and watch, and wait    
To notice how new things glisten     
As much, or more, than the late

Hard though it is to summon    
I hope peace may come in this way    
If full I trust in the promise     
That its truth will come day by day

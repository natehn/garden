The stone   
Just so    
Says to the earth    
“We are”

Balanced on its neighbors    
There is no conversation    
Just time    
Passing fast    
Seconds like centuries     
Rounding down    
Its little imperfections     

But it does have a voice    
It speaks, now and forever    
Of freedom    
Not from time    
Not from friction    
But from fear    
Fear that erodes far more    
Than just a little sediment    

Just be    
It says    
Be in the flow    
Where there is no need to fear    
For you are stone    
Shaped by time and friction    
Pleasures, and pains,     
And personalities    
But steady, always in yourself    
Not chasing    
Just being    
Being yourself    
Knowing yourself    
Being with the earth    

The stone speaks -    
If only we all could listen.

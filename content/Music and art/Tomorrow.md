*A living room in the forest.*

~

Sunday was    
Blue, lights, arrival, alone;     
Motorcycle, trees, zip ties, fear.    
So much fear.    
I called you over and over and over and you didn’t answer.    
Neither did Bonkers, or Manfred,    
Not for a while.    
Until eventually you did    
And I asked you what to do    
And you said    
You didn’t know    
But that we would figure it out    
Tomorrow.

Tomorrow was     
Slowness, questions, impatience, text threads;    
Moldy cheese, porch cat, noise show, sun.    
We all asked each other what to do    
And none of us knew    
Except you.    
We have to go back, you said.    
If we don’t go back, they win.    
We have to go back,    
To that place of fear and turmoil,    
And again make it     
A place of joy,    
A home.

Today is    
Pink, plastic, inflatable, couch;    
Living room, water buffalo, LARP-ers, alarm.    
You told me about Bonkers     
A real-life love triangle    
Right under my nose    
And they asked you what to do    
About the cruiser nearby    
And you told them.    
Then you asked me what to do    
And I said    
I didn’t know    
But that Bonkers is, well -     
Maybe it is better to focus    
On what you can control.

Tonight will be    
Bonfire, mandolin, train hop, folk song;    
Sundown, group dinner, cell phone, airport.    
Things will feel calm, in a way.    
I will choose to go.    
And we’ll ask each other what to do.    
And we will not know.    
But we will decide not to talk anymore:     
For our mutual safety,     
If not our friendship,    
It will be better to go silent.

And although tomorrow will be    
Failure, bulldozer, molotov, falsehood;    
Indictment, conspiracy, courthouse, RICO.    
Although tomorrow will be full of pain.    
And they will not let it go.    
I know you will survive    
For it is from you that I learned    
Of courage    
Of perseverance     
Of commitment    
And of purpose.    
It is because of you    
That I believe.

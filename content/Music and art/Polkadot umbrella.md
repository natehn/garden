Polkadot umbrella     
Draped over a linoleum floor     
The next stop is     
Hewes Street

Hewn from farmer folk     
This Hewitt, now harnessed     
To the city, to     
Global capital

This is a Manhattan bound      
M Local train     
The next stop is      
Marcy Avenue

It’s raining     
And he’s off     
Off to where? We wonder     
He knows?

He knows not     
Curiosity his curse     
The elevator     
Is at     
The center     
Of the platform

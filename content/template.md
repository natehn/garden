This is where I plant ideas. It's a little bit like a commonplace book, *biji*, memex, or public notebook. Unlike in a blog or other formally-published format, the thoughts shared here are **alive**. They are meant to be read, and more than that, to be engaged with, and to grow and change. There is no expectation that they be complete. They are *fragments*.

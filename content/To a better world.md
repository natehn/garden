When I took my gargantuan 1940s desktop typewriter to get repaired, my Lyft driver was fascinated. In a short, ten-minute ride, our conversation expanded from technology, to labor, to capitalism, and the future.

Once I arrived home, I improvised this poem for him - for Hamza, and called it "To a better world."

![[to-a-better-world.png|A scan of a typewritten poem, with handwritten edits. The poem text is included below.]]

~

**To a better world**

*for Hamza*

He hopes for a better world    
In the face of all this adversity   
From the seat of his black Toyota Camry   
He prays for the end of innocent deaths   
The lowering of prices   
The rising of wages   
The end of pollution of the planet   
He asks for EVERYTHING.   
And, if I had my way   
He would have it.

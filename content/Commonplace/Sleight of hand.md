Things here in America are..._not good_. We wonder whether our neighbors will be allowed to stay, and whether our international friends [will be allowed to return](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/160557/he-too). We look to our government in horror at its violence and repression. We wonder whether we should hunker down and survive, or run away, or [step out into the arena](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hfw536d_L_A) [to fight](https://cal.red/). (At least we are [no longer on our heels](https://crimethinc.com/2025/06/08/los-angeles-stands-up-to-ice-a-firsthand-report-on-the-clashes-of-june-6).) With the news hitting our neurons 24/7, it can hard not to feel the weight of the whole world, the weight of history, the weight of the future. It's _intense_.

How might we find joy in a time like this, without burying our heads in the sand?

An answer may lie in magic - in everyday sleight of hand. We all have the capacity for it: out of nothing, _something_. Something small. Something _beautiful_. Think of the singing abuelita in [Ross Gay's "Opera Singer."](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poems/92017/opera-singer) Or a picnic, under a tree, its [leaves dancing in the breeze](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/nmg36i1hy08cd5gn5xny5/2024-10-16-18.51.27.mov?rlkey=iqlax8wzn8gd1oq9eht1a5umj&st=cfg0qj9y&dl=0). [Saying "I LOVE YOU,"](https://www.are.na/block/24943609) maybe even [to a](https://sevengoodthings.com/with-that-moon-language-poem-by-hafiz/) [stranger](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/05/18/nyregion/metropolitan-diary.html?unlocked_article_code=1.QE8.O4ey.-OuyN58TbKhO&smid=url-share). The list goes on: [dad texts](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/06/13/well/family/dad-jokes-texts-fathers-day.html?rsrc=flt&unlocked_article_code=1.QE8.1saN.imCJRQQmaKsb&smid=url-share), [haiku](https://www.are.na/block/15853034), [gardening](https://bsky.app/profile/lifewinning.com/post/3ln4jl2llmc2c), [balloon animals](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/I6XJ_Bevajc). Mix together skill, wonder, an element of surprise, and _voilà_! You've all cracked a smile. In all this chaos, channel the trickster, and conjure "flowers" from thin air.

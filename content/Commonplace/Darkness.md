> The world is insane, and it will make the most transparently false case to you that it isn’t as it stares you in the face so plainly that it’s beyond comprehension. A place where you spiritually disconnect and have to laugh at what we as a species manage to do to each other. A place where you’re glad to feel like an alien, because fitting in sounds a crime against nature. That you’re not wrong, this doesn’t make sense. That this serves practically nobody, and yet here we are. There are folks who have made a life out of entertaining their worst and most perverse demons, and they’ve managed to stick you with the bill. I’m not making a case for nihilism, but a brief moment where you don’t feel the strain of needing to connect to anything and then you can get back to finding meaning with you’re feet on the ground.

Pile, 2026

~

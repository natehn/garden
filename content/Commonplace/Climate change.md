> even the rocks are dying. now that we’re here    
> in a cene of our making, time    
> to hold what’s left. palm sized & glossy    
> we call out to other    
> guts, flayed green crystal    
> planes of tempered stone.    
> we call out to each other then    
> 
> complain about the echo.    

“[even the rocks are dying](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/poetrymagazine/poems/1723537/even-the-rocks-are-dying),” by marion eames white

~

In my dreams, the world is not warming. We burn cretaceous plant matter by the barrel, and we pay nothing. Liquid gold begets authentic abundance; no games, no tricks. Treating the world like our plaything is without consequence. In my dreams, we expand without end, and we pay no price. 

*October 22, 2026*

~

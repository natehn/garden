Did past-me write this hierarchy of community needs? I'm not sure...it could also be from notes on someone else's talk, or from a group discussion. Nevertheless, here it is:

- The basic well-being of children and minors
- The basic well-being of elderly and disabled
- The basic well-being of caretakers
- The basic well-being of local workers
- The basic well-being of commuters
- A balanced, healthy non-human ecosystem
- A non-human ecosystem that involves humans symbiotically
- A beautiful community
- Collaborations with other communities
- Self-determination of the community
- Land ownership by the community
- The financial prosperity of the community

At the potluck the other day, we were talking about beauty. Beauty and awe. 

I was trying to think of what to share, and what ties them together.

I thought of the most powerful performance I’ve ever been part of, a performance of Britten’s *War Requiem* in college. It was a choir of 200 people and a full orchestra, and an audience of hundreds, and it was unlike anything I had experienced before. The music seemed to resonate to my very bones. And it was beautiful. It spoke to the deepest parts of the human experience, death, life, war, and peace. And it was ours - ours alone. And its moments of quiet, and of brilliant clangor, still live on in me today.

![[xr.png]]

Then I thought about activism. A different sort of beauty. Like when we blocked Times Square with a green boat that and a banner that said “ACT NOW.” And we sat in the street. And we said that business would not go on *as usual*. And I made a friend in the back row of the police bus who I still know today, years later. And we celebrated in the cell. And though I don’t always know what we accomplished, now, I remember feeling like I had done something, that we had done something, That was beautiful, too. 

![[phoenix park.png]]

And then I thought of an experience just a few weeks ago, in another part of the world, when I was walking and walking and walking, on an endless field of green, the dewy grass was soaking through my shoes, orange light shining over the berm of the hills ahead. I kept going; I chased the sun as it descended in the distance, and I thought of how it was on its way to my friends. And I said to them:

> I’m walking towards the retreating sun and realizing that it’s right over your head right now. I watched it dip away, and now its light is dying. But still it’s amazing, how far it reaches. And you’re a good chunk away around this big ol’ ball, and we’re catching light from the same star. 

And, yes, it was beautiful. To feel that you are part of something more, something greater, something beyond my own understanding - to have a sense that we are *one heart* - is the essence of beauty and awe.

Onward, with love.

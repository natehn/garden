At the potluck the other day, we were talking about beauty. Beauty and awe. 

I was trying to think of what to share, and what ties them together.

I thought of the most powerful performance I’ve ever been part of, a performance of Britten’s *War Requiem* in college. It was a choir of 200 people and a full orchestra, and an audience of hundreds, and it was powerful. The music seemed to resonate to my very bones. It was beautiful. It still resonates in me today.

Then I thought about activism. A different sort of beauty. Like when we blocked Times Square with a green boat that and a banner that said “ACT NOW.” And I made a friend in the back row of the police bus. And we celebrated in the cell. And I felt like I had done something, we had done something, and that was beautiful, too. 

![[xr.png]]

And I thought of when I was in another part of the world, not long ago. I was walking and walking and walking, on an endless field of green, the dewy grass was soaking through my shoes. I chased the sun as it descended, and I thought of how it was on its way to my friends, and I left voice messages with them, saying:

> I’m walking towards the retreating sun and realizing that it’s right over your head right now. I watched it dip away, and now its light is dying. But still it’s amazing, how far it reaches. And you’re a good chunk away around this big ol’ ball, and we’re catching light from the same star. 

And it was beautiful.

What ties these moments together? I think it is a feeling of being part of something greater than myself. Being part of a performance, a movement, a planet. A sense of being of *one heart*. What is more beautiful than that?

When I moved to the Twin Cities, and leaned into a new life, I somehow found myself at a song circle for the first time, and it has given me SO MUCH life. I hadn't really sung like this since I left the church 12 years ago and I am finding that to reconnect with my voice, and the experience of singing with others, has been a deep, resonant blessing in my life. 

The songs are sweet, simple, beautiful - toeing the line of the saccharine without giving me the ick. There's one sing I think of often, it goes: "You don't have to know the way / The way knows the way. / You don't have to find the way / Feel your way / Trust the way / The way knows / The way knows / The way knows the way." And with the passing of my grandmother this fall, I shared with my family a simple song of grief: "I don't know / If I can hold this alone / I need you / To sing this song." (There are so many more good ones, hmu for more.)

These songs allow us to cast our minds, collectively, toward healing, support, and a better vision for the future. And, they help me settle down into the unknown, into the change. (Change is the only truth, after all.)

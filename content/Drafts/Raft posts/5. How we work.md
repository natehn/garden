1. Embracing exclusion (JOMO)

Amazing people are spread all over the globe. There is simply no way for us to find times for virtual meetings for everyone. While we try to find times that work well for most people, we also embrace the fact that whoever shows up are the “right people,” and they will have a conversation that only they can have. This is also called “JOMO,” or the Joy Of Missing Out.
